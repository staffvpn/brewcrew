# Autumn seasonal blanket

Created in PixelLab (`create-image-pixen`), 17 November 2026.
Original transparent PNG: 64 × 80. Visible bounds: `[7, 4, 48, 72]`.
The original output is kept unchanged; the renderer uses the visible bounds.

A single soft terracotta, cream and sage tartan throw, with a folded top edge,
shaded fabric creases and short wool tassels. Dark walnut pixel outlines match
Brew Crew's warm cafe palette. The image contains only the fabric, so the game
keeps the player's selected chair and drapes the blanket over its back.

The same asset appears in the season reward, inventory and room. The garland
remains a separate decoration along the wall. Ownership, reward claims and
the existing +5% guest patience bonus are unchanged.
