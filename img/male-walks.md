# Male walking cycles

Created with PixelLab `transfer-outfit-v2`, 15 November 2026.
The approved female walk supplies the six walking poses and timing.
Each job uses one fixed direction and the corresponding male reference.

Characters: young male guest and older male guest with moustache and cap.
Directions: south, south-east, east, north-east, north; opposite sides mirror these.
60 new frames are appended to the embedded character atlas in `index.html`.
Every pre-existing atlas pixel, including all female artwork, is preserved.
Each cycle has a fixed foot anchor and pixel scale.
Guest eyes render directly from their sprite frames, without replacement overlays.
