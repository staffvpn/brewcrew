# Gold season espresso machine

Created with PixelLab `create-image-pixen` on 15 November 2026.
Original 96 × 80 transparent PNG; visible sprite bounds `[13, 10, 70, 63]`.

Art direction: straight-on symmetrical front elevation of a professional two-group
polished gold and brass espresso machine, emerald and walnut accents, two cups,
steam wands and gauges. Crisp warm cafe pixel art with dark brown outlines.
No side view, isometric perspective, text, pedestal or background.

The game uses the visible bounds for rendering without shrinking the machine
because of transparent margins. The PNG is the original PixelLab output.
