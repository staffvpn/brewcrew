# Season reward icons

Created with PixelLab `create-image-pixen` for Brew Crew, 15 November 2026.
48 × 48 PNG, transparent background, nearest-neighbor display.

- cash: coins and banknotes
- xp: coffee bean and experience sparkle
- supplies: coffee supply bundle
- boost: steaming cup with wings
- style: cafe interior
- staff: barista portrait
- badge: coffee medal
- machine: copper espresso machine
- pet: cat barista
- drink: seasonal cappuccino

Shared art direction: cozy cafe pixel art, walnut outline, cream, muted green,
terracotta and brass; centered inventory objects without text.
These icons illustrate existing rewards and do not change their values.
